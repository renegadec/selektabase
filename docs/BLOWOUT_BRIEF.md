## 2. Vision, Mission & Impact

- **Regional motorsport hub** — position Zimbabwe on the international map by hosting regional
  driftkhana and burnout competitions attracting premier drivers from across Southern Africa
  (South Africa, Botswana, Eswatini) alongside top Zimbabwean talent.
- **Tourism promotion** — drive domestic and cross-border sports tourism into Harare.
- **Youth empowerment & safe motorsport** — transition street-level burnouts into a regulated,
  enclosed, professionally stewarded motorsport environment that channels youth passion into
  automotive mechanics, driving skill and disciplined teamwork.
- **Philanthropy** — the festival frequently anchors charity campaigns (via the Selekta Base
  Foundation) to support vulnerable communities during year-end editions.

---

## 3. Festival Attractions & Experiences

An all-day, multi-activity experience catering to car enthusiasts and families alike.

### Motorsport & Action Arena
- **Driftkhana & burnouts** — high-intensity precision driving, tire-shredding donuts and drift
  battles featuring top regional and national car spinners.
- **Static car displays** — stance culture, custom engine builds, classic restorations and
  audio sound-off setups.
- **Biker showcase** — stunt riding, bike exhibitions and motorcycle convoys.

### Live Entertainment & Nightlife
- Performances by leading Zimbabwean and regional music artists.
- Top-tier DJ line-ups curated by Selekta Base and guest entertainers.
- Master of Ceremonies (MC) hypes and crowd interactions.
- Grand finale fireworks display.

### Family & Lifestyle Midway
- **Carnival & mini circus** — midway games, interactive contests, walk-around performers.
- **Kids' zone** — bouncing castles, inflatables and supervised children's games.
- **Food & concessions** — curated open-air food courts, beverage gardens, braai stations and
  cocktail bars.
- **Art & craft village** — local artisan pop-up stalls and apparel vendors.

## 4. Audience Demographics & Reach

- **Attendance:** 7,000+ attendees per edition.
- **Demographic spread:** multi-racial, multigenerational, ages 10–65.
- **Composition:** 80% Zimbabwean residents, 20% regional/international visitors and tourists.
- **Vibe & culture:** high disposable income youth, automotive enthusiasts, young professionals,
  urban families and corporate executives.

## 8a. Typography

- **Requested brand font:** **Ethnocentric** (Typodermic Fonts / Ray Larabie) — an expanded,
  squared techno display face. A good fit for the motorsport/"engineered performance" positioning.
- **Licensing status:** Ethnocentric Regular is free under the Typodermic *Desktop Licence* for
  logos, print, static graphics and rendered video, but that licence **excludes webfont
  embedding**. Serving it as live website text requires a MyFonts / Font Bros webfont licence.
  It is **not** in Typodermic's CC0 public-domain collection.
- **Shipped meanwhile:** **Neuropol** — same foundry and designer, same genre, released under
  **CC0 public domain**, so it is free to embed. The stack lists Ethnocentric first, so buying the
  licence and dropping `ethnocentric.woff2` into `public/fonts/` switches the site over with no
  code change.
- **Applied to the whole page:** by client request, one typeface covers every display element —
  headline, labels, ticker and stat figures. Neuropol ships a lowercase set matching its uppercase,
  so mixed-case text renders correctly where needed.
- **Body copy exception:** the description paragraph and the entire contact bar use **Poppins**
  (Indian Type Foundry, SIL OFL 1.1, self-hosted) at weights 300/400/500 — the wide techno face is
  unreadable at paragraph and small-text sizes, so running copy is deliberately set in a
  conventional geometric sans.
- **Action for client:** purchase the Ethnocentric webfont licence, or confirm Neuropol is
  acceptable.
